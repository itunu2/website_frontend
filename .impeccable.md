## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are potential clients evaluating whether to hire Itunu for B2B content strategy and writing.
- Secondary users are general visitors who should leave impressed by credibility, clarity, and professionalism.
- The page must drive confidence quickly and move visitors toward contact and conversion actions.

### Brand Personality
- Premium, human, decisive.
- Tone should feel expert and trustworthy without being stiff.
- Emotional goal: make visitors feel "this is the right person to trust with our messaging."

### Aesthetic Direction
- Direction: Canva-plus.
- Keep the reference structure and narrative flow, but elevate craft quality, spacing rhythm, typography hierarchy, and interactions.
- Motion style: subtle and intentional. No noisy or gimmicky animations.
- Anti-reference: anything that resembles generic AI-generated websites.

### Design Principles
1. Clarity before decoration: every section should communicate value instantly.
2. Premium restraint: bold hierarchy with controlled color and motion.
3. Real social proof: use authentic brand assets and credible visual treatment.
4. Cohesive rhythm: consistent spacing, radii, typography scales, and interaction states.
5. Invisible accessibility: keyboard, contrast, and reduced-motion quality are baseline, not optional.
